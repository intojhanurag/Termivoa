termivoa

/tɜːr·mɪ·ˈvoʊ·ə/ · noun

  1. your computer's terminal, on your phone, over a private network only you can reach.
  2. the way to answer your coding agent when it asks, from anywhere, in one tap.
Get the private beta then just pterm setup

Your phone talks to your computer. No one else is in the room.

Termivoa runs on your Mac or Linux box and is reached only through your own Tailscale network. There is no Termivoa server, no account and no open port.
